About VHELIBS

VHELIBS (Validation Helper for LIgands and Binding Sites) analyses protein–ligand complexes from PDB structures. It computes quality metrics (RSR, RSCC, occupancy, R-free, etc.) for ligands and their binding sites, and classifies them as Good, Dubious, or Bad based on user-defined thresholds.

Classification criteria

MetricDefault thresholdEffect on score
RSR< 0.24 → good, 0.24–0.40 → +1, > 0.40 → +2+1 or +2
RSCC> 0.9+1 if below
Occupancy= 1.0+1 if below
R-free< 1.0+1 if above
Tolerance2score ≤ tolerance → Dubious; > → Bad

Data sources

  • Structure files: RCSB PDB (mmCIF format)
  • Validation statistics: PDBe validation reports
  • Re-refined structures: PDB-REDO
  • 3D visualisation: NGL Viewer
